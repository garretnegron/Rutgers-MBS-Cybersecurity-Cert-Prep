# Security Controls

## Control Categories

Cybersecurity controls are grouped into three main categories:

- **Administrative/Managerial Controls**
- **Technical Controls**
- **Physical Controls**

### Administrative/Managerial Controls
Administrative controls address the human component of cybersecurity. These controls include policies and procedures that define how an organization manages data and clearly define employee responsibilities, including their role in protecting the organization.

While administrative controls are typically policy-based, enforcement may require the use of technical or physical controls.

### Technical Controls
Technical controls consist of solutions such as:

- Firewalls
- Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS)
- Intrusion Prevention Systems (IPS)
- Antivirus (AV) software
- Encryption

Technical controls are implemented to achieve various organizational security objectives.

### Physical Controls
Physical controls include:

- Door locks
- Cabinet locks
- Surveillance cameras
- Badge readers

These controls are used to limit physical access to assets by unauthorized personnel.

# Control Types

The primary control types include:

1. **Preventative**
2. **Corrective**
3. **Detective**
4. **Deterrent**

These controls work together to provide defense in depth and protect organizational assets.

### Preventative Controls
Designed to prevent an incident from occurring.

### Corrective Controls
Used to restore systems or assets after an incident.

### Detective Controls
Implemented to determine whether an incident has occurred or is currently in progress.

### Deterrent Controls
Designed to discourage attacks before they happen.

# Administrative Controls

| Control Name | Control Type | Control Purpose |
|-------------|-------------|----------------|
| Least Privilege | Preventative | Reduces risk and impact of malicious insiders or compromised accounts |
| Disaster Recovery Plans | Corrective | Provides business continuity and recovery after disruptions |
| Password Policies | Preventative | Reduces likelihood of account compromise through brute-force or dictionary attacks |
| Access Control Policies | Preventative | Protects confidentiality and integrity by defining who can access or modify data |
| Account Management Policies | Preventative | Manages account lifecycle, reduces attack surface, and limits risk from former employees or default accounts |
| Separation of Duties | Preventative | Reduces risk and impact of malicious insiders or compromised accounts |

---

# Technical Controls

| Control Name | Control Type | Control Purpose |
|-------------|-------------|----------------|
| Firewall | Preventative | Filters unwanted or malicious traffic from entering the network |
| IDS/IPS | Detective | Detects and prevents anomalous traffic matching known signatures or rules |
| Encryption | Deterrent | Provides confidentiality for sensitive information |
| Backups | Corrective | Restores and recovers systems after an event |
| Password Management | Preventative | Reduces password fatigue and promotes stronger credentials |
| Antivirus (AV) Software | Corrective | Detects and quarantines known threats |
| Manual Monitoring, Maintenance, and Intervention | Preventative | Identifies and manages threats, risks, and vulnerabilities in outdated systems |

---

# Physical Controls

| Control Name | Control Type | Control Purpose |
|-------------|-------------|----------------|
| Time-Controlled Safe | Deterrent | Reduces attack surface and impact from physical threats |
| Adequate Lighting | Deterrent | Deters threats by limiting places where attackers can hide |
| Closed-Circuit Television (CCTV) | Preventative / Detective | Acts as a deterrent and can be used to investigate incidents after they occur |
| Locking Cabinets (for Network Gear) | Preventative | Prevents unauthorized physical access to infrastructure equipment |
| Signage Indicating Alarm Service Provider | Deterrent | Makes attacks appear less likely to succeed |
| Locks | Deterrent / Preventative | Deters and prevents unauthorized physical access to assets |
| Fire Detection and Prevention Systems (Fire Alarms, Sprinklers, etc.) | Detective / Preventative | Detects fire and helps prevent damage to physical assets such as inventory and servers |
